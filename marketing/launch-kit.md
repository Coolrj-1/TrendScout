## Positioning

TrendScout is an open-source GitHub discovery radar for AI agents, MCP servers, local AI and developer tools. It records dated public repository snapshots and only reports seven-day star growth when a comparable historical snapshot exists.

Do not promise guaranteed discovery, investment returns, project quality, security, or real-time growth. Star totals are popularity signals, not momentum.
