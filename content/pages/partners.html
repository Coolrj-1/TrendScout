Our sponsorship standards

  • Every paid placement is prominently labelled Sponsored.
  • Payment never changes organic repository rankings, trend scores or editorial conclusions.
  • We only consider products relevant to developers and open-source builders.
  • We may decline campaigns that are misleading, unsafe or unrelated to the audience.
  • We do not promise clicks, conversions, impressions or a particular audience size.

TrendScout is an early-stage project. We are building reliable audience measurement and will share only verified analytics for a clearly stated period and metric. Request counts are not unique visitors.
